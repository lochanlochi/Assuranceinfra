Who we are

Built in layers, like the earth itself.

Rammed earth is made by compressing one layer at a time until the wall is stronger than any single layer. We build the same way — design, approvals, structure, finish — each stage checked before the next one begins.

Questions

Clear answers, before you ask.

Still wondering about something? Call us on +91 74071 11999.

Will I get the right price?

Yes. Pricing is fixed before we start and stays the same until the project is finished — no mid-way escalations.

Will I get the materials that are promised?

Every material and brand is written into the agreement and can be verified on site at any stage.

Who will design my home?

Our architects, each with over 10 years of experience. You can make unlimited changes for free before construction begins.

Who will execute the project?

A site engineer, head of execution, quality-control lead and project manager oversee every build together.

Will my project be finished on time?

Yes. Our agreement includes a delay clause that levies a penalty on us if we miss your handover date.
