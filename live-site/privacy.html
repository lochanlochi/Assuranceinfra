Privacy policy.

What we collect when you contact us through this website, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Last updated: 28 October 2026

1. Who we are

This website is operated by Assurance Infra, 56/1, Sri Krishna Towers, 9th Main Rd, Jayanagar 5th Block, Bengaluru 560041. For any privacy question, write to support@assuranceinfra.com.

2. Information we collect

  • Enquiry and estimate forms: your name, phone number, email (optional), plot location and size, project type, preferred package, timeline and any message you add.
  • Website chat assistant: the questions you type and, if you request a call back, your name and phone number.
  • WhatsApp, email and phone: the messages you send us and your contact details. Calls to our team may be recorded.
  • Campaign source: if you arrive from one of our advertisements, the campaign tags in the link (for example the ad source), so we know which campaigns are useful.
  • Technical data: our hosting provider keeps standard server logs (such as IP address, browser type and pages requested) for security and reliability.

3. How we use it

We use this information only to respond to your enquiry, arrange consultations and site visits, prepare estimates, deliver and support the services you engage us for, keep records of our communications, and understand which of our campaigns help people find us. We collect user data such as your name and contact information to provide our construction, interior design and renovation services.

4. How your information reaches us

Our enquiry forms and chat hand-off open WhatsApp with your details filled in; nothing is sent until you press send. WhatsApp is operated by Meta and its own privacy policy applies to messages sent through it. The chat assistant may also pass your questions to our team so we can follow up. Your browser keeps your chat conversation only for the current browsing session.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with project partners and professionals (such as architects, engineers or contractors) where needed to deliver your project, with service providers that run this website and our communications, or where required by law.

6. Third-party services on this site

This website uses Google Maps (on the Contact page) and Google Fonts, and is hosted on Vercel. These providers may receive technical information such as your IP address when the page loads, under their own privacy policies.

7. Retention and security

We keep your information only as long as needed for the purposes above, or as required for legal, tax and accounting records, and we take reasonable measures to protect it.

8. Your choices

You may ask us to access, correct or delete your personal information, or to stop contacting you, by writing to support@assuranceinfra.com. We will respond as required under applicable Indian law, including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always available on this page. See also our Terms of Use and Disclaimer.
